I am a graduate researcher at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en){:target="_blank"}, where I completed my master's in Computer Science with [Guillaume Lajoie](https://www.guillaumelajoie.com/){:target="_blank"} and [Dhanya Sridhar](https://www.dsridhar.com/){:target="_blank"}.

I'm broadly interested in open-ended learning, particularly how systems can build on what they already know, reusing and composing prior knowledge to acquire new skills. Drawing insights from algorithmic information theory and cognitive science, my recent contributions have focused on formalizing compositional structure and using it to guide adaptive curricula.
