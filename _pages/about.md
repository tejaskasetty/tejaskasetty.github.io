I am a graduate researcher at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en){:target="_blank"} and [Université de Montréal](https://www.umontreal.ca/){:target="_blank"}, where I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science under the supervision of [Guillaume Lajoie](https://www.guillaumelajoie.com/){:target="_blank"} and [Dhanya Sridhar](https://www.dsridhar.com/){:target="_blank"}. 

My research interests include learning systems that can continually acquire, organize, and reuse knowledge. I am particularly interested in compositional representations, curriculum and data selection, and understanding learning and generalization, through an information-theoretic lens.

Outside of research, I enjoy chess, climbing, snowboarding, and playing football.
