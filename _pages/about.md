I am a graduate researcher at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en){:target="_blank"} and [Université de Montréal](https://www.umontreal.ca/){:target="_blank"}, where I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science under the supervision of [Guillaume Lajoie](https://www.guillaumelajoie.com/){:target="_blank"} and [Dhanya Sridhar](https://www.dsridhar.com/){:target="_blank"}. 

I am broadly interested in open-ended intelligence: how learning systems can continually discover, organize, and reuse knowledge to build increasingly structured representations, skills, and reasoning strategies over time. My work sits at the intersection of continual learning, compositionality, adaptive curricula and data selection, and multi-agent learning, drawing on information-theoretic and cognitive perspectives to study how knowledge is acquired and how capabilities emerge, accumulate, and spread across interacting agents.

Outside of research, I enjoy chess, climbing, snowboarding, and playing football.
